# Data card

Everything ShadowFlow runs on is generated by `backend/generate_data.py`.
No real bank data is used or included anywhere in this repository.

## How it is made

- **Generator:** Python `random` (seed 42 by default) plus NumPy RNG, both
  seeded, so the committed dataset is byte-for-byte reproducible
  (`make data` regenerates it).
- **Window:** 90 days, 2026-01-01 to 2026-03-31, three fictional banks
  (BankA, BankB, BankC).
- **Accounts:** 400 per bank (1,635 in total including ring accounts) across
  persons, employers, shops, landlords, utilities, HQs and mule/collector
  roles.
- **Benign traffic:** salaries, shop card payments, rent, utilities, P2P
  transfers, employer expenses and inter-bank moves, with log-normal amounts
  and jittered times. ~70,021 payments in the seed-42 dataset.
- **Files:** `transactions_Bank{A,B,C}.csv` with columns `txn_id`,
  `timestamp`, `src_account`, `dst_account`, `amount`, `bank_src`,
  `bank_dst`, `channel`; cross-bank payments appear in both banks' files and
  are deduplicated by `txn_id` on load.
- **Benchmark noise levels:** low = 0.5x, medium = 1.0x, high = 1.5x the
  background benign volume only (see [BENCHMARK](BENCHMARK.md)).

## What is injected (ground truth)

Recorded in `ground_truth.json` with account lists and transaction ids:

- **5 money cycles** - funds return to the origin within 48h minus a fee;
- **5 mule chains** - 4-7 accounts forwarding 90-98% within 1-90 minutes;
- **5 smurfing rings** - 15-40 deposits just under 10,000 into one
  collector, then consolidated out;
- **7 decoys** that are deliberately benign: a busy shop, a large payroll,
  a landlord, a festival cash kitty, a payout float, and a seasonal loop
  (two accounts) - the last two are the look-alikes that still get flagged.

Hardening details: hop delays and amounts carry jitter, some hops are
"hidden" (0-2 minute delays, like one batch transfer seen as two edges), and
ring accounts also lead ordinary lives so the benign filter cannot separate
them by traffic volume alone.

The ground-truth labels are **stripped from the CSVs** before writing
(`pattern_meta` is dropped): the detector only ever reads the same columns a
bank would hand over.

## What is not modelled

- No balances, accounts, or ledger - only payment flows. Overdrafts,
  intra-day balances and settlement do not exist.
- No day-of-week, holiday or seasonal calendar effects.
- No currencies, FX, cards-as-accounts, or correspondent banking layers.
- No KYC, customer names, or any personally identifiable information.
- No SAR/STR reporting workflow, regulatory thresholds other than the fixed
  10,000 structuring threshold, or currency-transaction reports.
- No cash transactions except as generated channel labels; no hawala or
  trade-based laundering (only the three injected families).
- No evolving adversary in the data itself: the adversary lab composes new
  schemes at query time instead.
- No measurement noise from real ingestion: no missing rows, duplicates (by
  id), clock skew or account-closure events.

## Regenerating

```bash
make data      # regenerate seed-42 data + run the pipeline
make benchmark # 10 seeds x 3 noise levels -> docs/BENCHMARK.md
```
